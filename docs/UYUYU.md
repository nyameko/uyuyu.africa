# UY

## UY.AFRICA

UY is the public identity.

It is short, memorable, technology-agnostic and deliberately open to interpretation.

UY represents a broader family of African and Global South ideas around knowledge, capability, community, action and prosperity.

The organisation is:

> **UY.AFRICA**

---

# UYUYU

## The UYUYU Symbol

**UYUYU** is the visual and cultural symbol of UY.

It is derived from the repeated U/Y structure found in the five foundational concepts:

**Ulwazi · Yenza · Ubuntu · Yumn (يُمن) · Uwezo**

The symbol is intentionally abstract.

It can be interpreted as:

* people standing together;
* people raising their hands;
* people building together;
* people holding something up collectively;
* a network of individuals;
* an interconnected structure;
* a repeating pattern;
* a fractal;
* or an infinite network.

The symbol does not have a single prescribed interpretation.

Its meaning emerges from the community.

---

# The Fractal

UYUYU can be repeated indefinitely:

```text
UY
UYUYU
UYUYUYUYU
UYUYUYUYUYUYUYU
UYUYUYUYUYUYUYUYUYUY...
```

The pattern represents the fundamental scaling mechanism of UY.

One person becomes a team.

A team becomes a club.

A club becomes a community.

Communities connect into cities.

Cities connect into countries.

Countries connect across Africa.

Africa connects with the Global South and the world.

There is no fixed boundary.

> **One UY becomes many.**

> **Many UYs become a network.**

> **The network has no edge.**

# The Five Principles

## ULWAZI

### Knowledge

Knowledge is the beginning.

Curiosity, learning, understanding, science, mathematics, technology and shared experience create the foundation from which capability can grow.

---

## YENZA

### Build · Create · Make

Knowledge becomes meaningful when we use it.

UY encourages people to build things, experiment, solve problems, make mistakes, collaborate and create.

We learn by doing.

---

## UBUNTU

### I Am Because We Are

Ubuntu is the centre of the UY philosophy.

Capability does not exist in isolation.

We learn from one another.

We build together.

We create opportunities for one another.

We succeed together.

> **I am because we are.**

---

## YUMN

### Prosperity · Good Fortune · Success

The purpose of capability and collective action is not merely technical achievement.

It is to create opportunity, prosperity and a better future for ourselves, our communities, our continent and the wider world.

**Yumn — يُمن** represents good fortune, blessing, prosperity and success.

---

## UWEZO

### Capability · Ability · Power

Knowledge becomes capability.

Capability creates agency.

Capability gives people the power to solve problems, create opportunities, conduct research and build new futures.

---

# The UY Cycle

The five principles are not a rigid sequence.

They are interconnected and cyclical.

They may begin from any point:

```text
             ULWAZI
            KNOWLEDGE
                │
                ↓
              YENZA
        BUILD · CREATE · MAKE
                │
                ↓
              UBUNTU
        I AM BECAUSE WE ARE
                │
                ↓
               YUMN
       PROSPERITY · SUCCESS
                │
                ↓
              UWEZO
       CAPABILITY · POWER
                │
                └──────────────→ ULWAZI
```

Another activation may begin with Ubuntu.

Another with Yenza.

Another with Uwezo.

The order can change.

The principles reinforce one another.

**Ubuntu remains the centre.**

# For Us. By Us. With Us.

UY is built around participation.

Young people are not merely recipients of programmes.

They are:

* builders;
* competitors;
* organisers;
* researchers;
* teachers;
* mentors;
* volunteers;
* entrepreneurs;
* founders;
* and eventually supporters of the next generation.

UY is:

> **For us. By us. With us.**

---

# Each One, Teach One

UY adopts the principle:

> ## **Each One, Teach One**

What we learn, we share.

What we build, we teach.

What we receive, we pass forward.

Every participant is both a learner and a potential teacher.

A student can become a mentor.

A competitor can become a coach.

A researcher can become a teacher.

A founder can create opportunities for another founder.

No one is expected to know everything.

No one is expected to teach everything.

But everyone has something they can teach, and everyone has something they can learn.

> **Knowledge travels through the community.**

> **Each one teaches one. Together, we teach millions.**

# What UY Is

UY is not a conventional university.

It is not a conventional research institute.

It is not a technology vendor.

It is not a Raspberry Pi organisation.

It is not a formal training provider.

It is not an exclusive technology club.

UY is an **open, independent, African network for activation, capability, research and entrepreneurship.**

UY creates pathways through which young people can move from curiosity to capability, from capability to research and entrepreneurship, and from achievement to giving back.

> **UY is the pipeline and connective fabric.**
