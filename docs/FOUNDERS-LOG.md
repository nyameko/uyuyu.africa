# 2026-08-31

## UY concept formalised.

* Original working identity:
One Million Raspberry Pis Africa.

* Refactored to:
One Million Research Activations.

* Founding programme:
One Million Raspberry Pis.

* Core philosophy:
Knowledge → Capability → Action.

* Key principles:
Ubuntu
Ulwazi
Uwezo
Yenza

* Technology:
Platform agnostic.

* Operating model:
Open by default.

* Strategic role:
Enable, connect, support and amplify existing African
technology, education and research ecosystems.

* Long-term:
Build a pipeline from undergraduate exploration,
through infrastructure and research, into entrepreneurship,
and eventually alumni contribution back into the ecosystem.

---

# Founding UY Africa

This repository documents the creation of UY Africa
from its earliest conceptual stage.

* Nothing here is presented as a finished institutional
model.

* Ideas will change.

* Programmes will change.

* Governance will evolve.

* Technologies will change.

The objective is to document the process openly so that
others can learn from our successes and failures.
